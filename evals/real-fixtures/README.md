# Legally reusable local speech fixtures

These short interview-style fixtures are generated by `scripts/generate-real-fixtures.ps1` from the audio fixtures distributed in the MIT-licensed `SYSTRAN/faster-whisper` repository, then wrapped in simple FFmpeg video. The source commit and repository license are recorded in `docs/upstream-study.md`. Preserve the upstream attribution when redistributing them. They exercise:

- `one-speaker.mp4`: one speaker with natural pauses.
- `two-speaker-overlap.mp4`: the upstream stereo diarization fixture.
- `background-music-proper-nouns.mp4`: the upstream hotwords/proper-nouns fixture mixed with low-level background tone and changing video.

They are small developer fixtures, not quality benchmarks for natural human speech. Level-2 faster-whisper/WhisperX runs are optional and should record the installed model/runtime in the benchmark report.
